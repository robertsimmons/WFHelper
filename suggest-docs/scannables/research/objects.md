# Objects and Plants

Codex Objects entries: fish, containers, mission props, defense targets, Ayatan treasures and plants. Each fills at 1 to 20 scans and gives only Codex progress.

**Pain level: brutal, long tail.** Most entries need 12 scans and the game counts one scan per object per mission, so the grind is the number of runs. The real bottlenecks are the rare spawns: bait-only fish, rare and reinforced containers (about 2.5% of missions combined), and Dark Refractory loot rooms.

## Gates
- Vor's Prize (Cetus fish, Plains of Eidolon).
- Vox Solaris (Fortuna fish, Orb Vallis).
- Heart of Deimos (Cambion Drift fish and containers).
- Whispers in the Walls (Albrecht's Laboratories).
- Angels of the Zariman (Zariman Ten Zero).
- The Old Peace (Descendia and Perita Rebellion).
- Ostron standing: Offworlder for Twilight Bait, Trusted for Norg Bait, Surah for Cuthol Bait, Kin for Glappid Bait.
- Solaris United standing: Rapscallion for Longwinder Bait, Doer for Charamote Bait, Cove for Synathid Bait.
- Entrati standing: Associate for Processed Fass Residue, Friend for Processed Vome Residue.

## Gear
- Helios with Investigator scans every unfinished entry it passes, but each auto-scan spends a Codex Scanner charge (near-unanimous). Bring a stack of Codex Scanners, or a Synthesis Scanner with the Sol-Battery widget for infinite charges.
- Golden Instinct (Helminth ability) pings Ayatan sculptures and rare or reinforced containers; the wiki's own trick is to cast it at the start of a Capture and abort if nothing lights up. It does not detect Molted Casks or an already-opened container.
- Loot Detector, Thief's Wit and Animal Instinct show containers and sculptures on the radar (wiki).
- Oxylus with Scan Aquatic Lifeforms, Luminous Dye (100 standing) and Banshee's Sonar make fish visible (wiki).
- Container-breaking range (Limbo's Cataclysm, Xaku's Vast Untime, Nyx's Absorb) clears Ayatan stars out of crates faster (wiki).

## Places
### Plains of Eidolon: Earth, Cetus nodes. Yield 8 per run.
- Best run: Cetus on Earth. Free-roam with no timer or objective; fish need Cetus standing and bait.
1. Buy a Fishing Spear from Fisher Hai-Luk, plus Twilight Bait (Karkina, Sharrac), Norg Bait, Cuthol Bait and Glappid Bait as your rank allows.
2. Pull the spear out first, because fish only spawn while it is equipped. Then swap to a scanner and scan each fish as it swims. You do not need to catch it. Each fish scans once, so a species with 12 scans needs 12 separate fish.
3. Ponds (day): Yogwun, Khut-Khut, Mortus Lungfish. Mortus Lungfish is common at night with Peppered Bait. Cuthol is a pond hotspot fish at night (Cuthol Bait at a white-bubble hotspot).
4. Gara Toht Lake directly north of Cetus: Norg at night, Norg Bait at a hotspot.
5. Ocean coastlines far left and right of the Cetus gate: Sharrac and Karkina (Twilight Bait, day or night), Glappid at night (Glappid Bait). The step people get wrong: the rare fish only appear at a hotspot after you throw the bait, so drop it on the bubbling patch first.
6. Do not wade into the water; it scares fish off and at night applies a weak Magnetic proc.
7. Imperial Rampart: scan the emplacement at a Grineer camp, for instance the one north of the whirlpool south-west of Cetus.
8. Grineer Supply Cache: appears in Cache Recovery bounty stages here; scan it before opening.
- Items here: Khut-Khut, Yogwun, Mortus Lungfish, Cuthol, Norg, Sharrac, Karkina, Glappid, Imperial Rampart, Grineer Supply Cache.

### Orb Vallis: Venus, Fortuna nodes. Yield 8 per run.
- Best run: Fortuna on Venus. Free-roam; warm weather lasts only 6 min 40 s of each 26 min 40 s cycle, so time the warm-only fish.
1. Buy a Fishing Spear (or Shock Spear) from The Business plus Broad-Spectrum Bait, Narrow-Spectrum Bait, Kriller Bait, Longwinder Bait, Charamote Bait and Synathid Bait by rank.
2. Equip the spear to spawn fish, swap to the scanner, scan.
3. Coolant Reservoir lake west of Fortuna: Echowinder and Kriller (warm), Sapcaddy (cold), Longwinder (warm hotspot, Longwinder Bait).
4. Landlocked ponds: Brickie and Eye-Eye (warm weather).
5. Caves with water: Charamote and Synathid are rare cave-hotspot fish (Charamote Bait, Synathid Bait; Synathid prefers warm weather). Throw the bait on the hotspot sheen, not the open water.
6. Gorgaricus Sac: the purple sacs hanging under giant mushroom caps. Scan, then hit it for spores. If it bursts empty, play solo.
- Items here: Echowinder, Kriller, Sapcaddy, Longwinder, Brickie, Eye-Eye, Charamote, Synathid, Gorgaricus Sac.

### Cambion Drift: Deimos, Necralisk nodes. Yield 7 fish per run, plus containers.
- Best run: free-roam on Deimos, run it in the Vome cycle for the Vome-only fish.
1. Fass lasts about 100 min and Vome about 50 min of each 150 min cycle. Glutinox, Aquapulmo and Myxostomata only appear during Vome.
2. Collect residue first: Fass Residue (orange zones during Vome, break the deposits) and Vome Residue (blue zones during Fass). Processed Fass and Vome Residue are bought from Entrati.
3. Surface hotspots: Glutinox (Fass Residue, Vome), Aquapulmo (Processed Fass Residue, Vome).
4. Caves: Amniophysi (common, no bait), Vitreospina (Fass Residue), Duroid and Chondricord (Vome Residue at cave hotspots), Myxostomata (Processed Vome Residue, Vome, cave hotspot).
5. Break Entrati ruin caskets: Sagiant Entrati Casket is common near the Requiem Obelisks; Auroron Entrati Casket and Spitia Infested Cyst can rarely replace a Sagiant or Rolizor cyst, so scan every cyst and casket you pass.
6. Isolation Vault Container: each Isolation Vault holds 14, so one vault covers the 12 scans.
- Items here: Amniophysi, Vitreospina, Duroid, Chondricord, Glutinox, Aquapulmo, Myxostomata, Sagiant Entrati Casket, Auroron Entrati Casket, Spitia Infested Cyst, Isolation Vault Container.

### Albrecht's Laboratories: Deimos, Albrecht's Laboratories nodes and Netracells. Yield 1 to 2 per run.
- Best run: a Netracell. Its Entrati Netracell Coffer counts toward the Muzureh entry when scanned.
1. Run a Netracell and scan the large coffer before you open it.
2. In ordinary Albrecht's missions, a Thalmin Entrati Coffer is the reinforced roll and a Muzureh Entrati Coffer the rare roll. They make no humming sound and Golden Instinct does not mark them.
3. Scan before you break it; Thalmin leaves nothing behind to scan afterwards.
- Items here: Muzureh Entrati Coffer, Thalmin Entrati Coffer.

### Zariman Ten Zero: Zariman nodes (Halako Perimeter, Everview Arc, The Greenway, Tuvul Commons, Oro Works) and Chrysalith bounties. Yield 1 to 3 per run.
- Best run: Halako Perimeter, Exterminate. Short, and the map has the hatch covers.
1. Hatch Cover: in the room with a large hole in the middle like an elevator shaft, jump up to the second floor; several sit in that room and the next, all connected.
2. Find a small glowing Globe Light on a table or bench and interact with it. A ghost-child runs to the nearest item of interest, including Ayatan sculptures and Molted Casks. Follow it.
3. The Molted Cask (rare) and Ancient Molted Cask (reinforced) are random per mission. Golden Instinct does not mark them; the Lost Spirit is the way to them. Collect all Voidplumes and Zarium Accolades before using the globe so it points at the cask.
- Items here: Hatch Cover, Molted Cask, Ancient Molted Cask.

### Dark Refractory: The Descendia and The Perita Rebellion (Navigation, Dark Refractory). Yield 0 to 1 per run.
- Best run: The Descendia, reach a Plunder or Plunder Roulette room. It is the only place the Hoards appear and the room gives 30 seconds of box-breaking.
1. In a Plunder room, scan every container before you break it. Raro Hoard and Inusitatus Hoard are the rare and reinforced rolls.
2. In The Perita Rebellion, break and scan every storage container you pass for the Standard and Abundant Prime Grineer Munitions Boxes. No fixed spot; players who tried the whole Codex call these among the hardest.
3. Helios with Investigator picks up the scan while you work.
- Items here: Raro Hoard, Inusitatus Hoard, Standard Prime Grineer Munitions Box, Abundant Prime Grineer Munitions Box.

### Grineer Sealab: Uranus (Sycorax, Stephano, Ophelia, Umbriel, Ariel, Caliban, Desdemona, Titania, Rosalind, Puck, Cressida) and Earth Mariana. Yield 3 to 4 per run.
- Best run: Puck, Uranus, Exterminate (Lanx vents spawn there) or Ariel, Capture (flooded room with the barrels).
1. Drekar Blunt: kill Grineer Lancers, Troopers and similar units until they deploy the blue-grey Blunts. Blunts only start spawning around level 10, so Sealab levels are fine.
2. Lanx: scan the shark swimming in the submersible areas; avoid bumping into it.
3. Soag Domestik Drone: the Grineer vacuum that wanders the tile.
4. Large Grineer Exploding Barrel: on Caliban (Rescue) or Ariel (Capture), follow the waterfall stairs down underwater and turn around; three barrels sit upper left.
- Items here: Drekar Blunt, Lanx, Soag Domestik Drone, Large Grineer Exploding Barrel.

### Grineer Settlement: Mars (Tharsis, Hellas, Spear, Alator, Arval, Ares, Augustus, Ara, Martialis, War, Olympus). Yield 1 per run.
- Best run: Spear, Mars, Defense. Short, scan the Blunt when a Grineer drops it.
1. Scan the sandy Arid Blunt when a Grineer deploys one.
- Items here: Arid Blunt.

### Kuva Fortress: Kuva Fortress nodes (Dakata, Koro, Nabuk, Rotuma, Pago, Garus, Taveuni). Yield 3 per run.
- Best run: Dakata, Exterminate. Short and the tile holds the fortress props.
1. Break and scan Polished Storage Crates, which replace the usual Grineer crates.
2. Scan the Shrapnel Mines before they trigger; destroy them from range.
3. Scan the Imperial Rampart emplacements and the Large Grineer Exploding Barrels in the Fortress corridors.
- Items here: Polished Storage Crate, Shrapnel Mine, Imperial Rampart, Large Grineer Exploding Barrel.

### Grineer Asteroid: Mercury (Caloris, Lares, Elion, M Prime, Boethius), Saturn (Enceladus, Dione, Rhea, Numa, Keeler), Ceres Draco. Yield 3 per run.
- Best run: Lares, Mercury, Defense (Infested). The objective is the Orokin Power Core.
1. Orokin Power Core: scan the reactor at the start of the Defense. One scan per mission, so 20 are 20 runs; stealth is no help against an object.
2. Mining Machine: Enceladus (Saturn), Sabotage. Scan every machine at the target spots.
3. Fans: the broken fan doorway; any Grineer Asteroid, Galleon or Shipyard.
- Items here: Orokin Power Core, Mining Machine, Fan, Large Grineer Exploding Barrel.

### Grineer Galleon: Earth Pacific, Mercury (Pantheon, Odin, Terminus, Suisei), Mars Vallis, Ceres Thon, Saturn (Cassini, Helene, Titan, Calypso). Yield 1 to 2 per run.
- Best run: Terminus, Mercury, Sabotage (reactor room with the Core Pipes), or Pacific, Earth, Rescue (fan).
1. Core Pipe: in a Sabotage at the reactor, the four corner pillars around it hold pipes. Players report you only get scannable pipes on the coolant route (put the Coolant in the fuel core instead of the injector).
2. Fan: in Pacific, Earth, Rescue, a tunnel inside the prison cell block has one.
- Items here: Core Pipe, Fan.

### Grineer Forest: Earth (E Prime, Gaia, Lith, Everest, Mantle, Cambria, Eurasia, Cervantes, Oro). Yield 3 to 4 per run.
- Best run: Everest, Earth, Excavation, for Excavators; Cervantes, Sabotage for the Toxin Injector.
1. Excavator: start an Excavation and keep powering new dig sites (a site appears about every 60 seconds, up to three at once). Scan each Excavator as it deploys. A single run can cover most of the 20.
2. Toxin Injector: Cervantes Sabotage, at the Toxin Injector after the biocatalyst stage. Scan it before the defense ends.
3. Moonlight Jadeleaf: night only. Grineer Forest uses the same day/night cycle as the Plains. Scan the plants near grass or water in E Prime or Cervantes at night; Oxylus Botanist loots them.
4. Forgotten Grineer Storage Container: a rare Earth container (mossy and glowing). Random per mission, scan it before opening.
- Items here: Excavator, Toxin Injector, Moonlight Jadeleaf, Forgotten Grineer Storage Container.

### Corpus tilesets: Corpus Gas City (Jupiter), Corpus Ship (Venus, Phobos), Corpus Ice Planet (Europa), Archwing Rush on Phobos Kepler. Yield 3 to 5 per run.
- Best run: Carme, Jupiter, Mobile Defense (Corpus Systems and Distressed Pipes) or Kepler, Phobos, Rush (Transport and Stasis Mines).
1. Corpus Systems: each data terminal in a Corpus Mobile Defense counts. Scan every terminal you carry the datamass to.
2. Distressed Pipe: orange-lit wall and ceiling pipes in Gas City tiles. Scan, then shoot one to see the flame jet. Each pipe scans once per mission.
3. Warframe Cryopod: scan the cryopod at the start of any Defense on a Grineer, Corpus or Orokin tileset, then abort. One scan per mission, so run quick Defense nodes. A Sealab excavation-site Defense has four cryopods.
4. Large Corpus Exploding Barrel: found where blowing it opens a path, on Corpus Ship, Corpus Ice Planet and Infested Ship. Only a couple per mission, so 11 more scans take several runs.
5. Transport and Corpus Stasis Mine: Archwing Rush, Kepler. Three transports per run; mines line the route to the loading dock.
- Items here: Corpus Systems, Distressed Pipe, Warframe Cryopod, Large Corpus Exploding Barrel, Transport, Corpus Stasis Mine.

### Infested Ship: Eris (Naeglar, Brugia, Oestrus, Saxis, Solium, Isos), Europa Armaros. Yield 2 per run.
- Best run: Oestrus, Eris, Infested Salvage, for Reinforced Glass; Naeglar, Eris, Sabotage for the tumors.
1. Reinforced Glass: scan the three panes that spawn on one of the two possible Oestrus maps. A run covers three of the 12.
2. Infested Tumor: in a Hive Sabotage or Rescue, five tumors surround the hive. The scanner highlights them through walls. A second Jordas Verdict variant of the entry no longer exists.
- Items here: Reinforced Glass, Infested Tumor.

### Orokin Void: Void (Taranis, Hepit, Teshub, Ani, Tiwaz and the rest). Yield 2 per run.
- Best run: Hepit, Void, Capture (Orokin Tower). Short, with a Death Orb trap room on the way.
1. Death Orb: scan the large trap orbs in the Orokin Tower rooms. A Sealab dead-end tile has a disassembled one too.
2. Argon Pegmatite: the Argon Crystal deposit, found only in the Void. Five scans; a run usually has one or two.
- Items here: Death Orb, Argon Pegmatite.

### Quest replays: Codex, then replay quest. Yield 1 per run.
- Best run: replay each from the quest's Codex entry.
1. Arcane Machine: replay The New Strange; the machine you defend in the Derelict stage (Ose, Europa, Defeat Chroma) counts. The unscannable one is in Stolen Dreams.
2. Cascade Bomb: replay Once Awake, final stage (Lares, Mercury, defend the bomb). Scan the power core.
- Items here: Arcane Machine, Cascade Bomb.

### Any mission: rare containers, Ayatan treasures, resource deposits. Yield 0 to 1 per run.
- Best run: Capture or Exterminate on a short tileset with Golden Instinct and a loot-radar companion.
1. Rare and reinforced Grineer, Corpus and Orokin containers: a rare roll in about 2% of missions, reinforced in a further 0.5%. They glow orange and hum. Special missions, Syndicate missions and Dark Sectors raise the odds. Scan the Corpus ones before you break them; they leave nothing to scan afterwards. Grineer containers and rare and reinforced Orokin ones stay scannable after opening.
2. Ayatan sculptures: each mission has a 1 in 7 chance of one hidden like a Syndicate medallion. Maroo's Weekly Ayatan Treasure Hunt (Alerts window, parkour room) guarantees one and rotates Sah, Ayr, Orta, Vaya, Piv, Valana in a six-week loop. Sculptures look like normal pickups; the scanner highlights them.
3. Ayatan stars: break any storage container or locker, or kill and scan Kuaka on the Plains. Five scans each; scanning stars in your Orbiter does not count, so do it in a mission.
4. Resource deposits: Gallium Deposit on Mars and Uranus, Neural Array on Jupiter and Kuva Fortress. Five scans each; deposits only appear where the planet's rare resource drops.
- Items here: Rare Grineer Storage Container, Rare Corpus Storage Container, Rare Orokin Storage Container, Reinforced Grineer Storage Container, Reinforced Corpus Storage Container, Reinforced Orokin Storage Container, Ayatan Ayr Sculpture, Ayatan Orta Sculpture, Ayatan Piv Sculpture, Ayatan Sah Sculpture, Ayatan Vaya Sculpture, Ayatan Amber Star, Ayatan Cyan Star, Gallium Deposit, Neural Array.

## Gotchas
- One scan per object per mission, so a 20-scan entry is 20 separate objects or 20 missions.
- Fish scan while swimming, only exist while a spear is out, and scatter if you wade in or miss. Rare fish need bait at a hotspot.
- Corpus rare and reinforced containers and Thalmin coffers vanish when broken. Scan first.
- Vome-only fish (Glutinox, Aquapulmo, Myxostomata) are gone for 100 of every 150 minutes.
- Orb Vallis warm weather is the short 6 min 40 s window.
- Anarch turrets cannot be scanned and Anasa has no working scan source.
- Scanning a sculpture in your Orbiter no longer counts.
- Stars scan only in missions, five times each.
- Quest replays skip the rewards but still give the scans. Hidden Messages, Angels of the Zariman and Vor's Prize cannot be replayed.

## Community sentiment
Players who chase the whole Codex call it unfinishable; several entries are bugged or removed. Rare and reinforced containers are the commonly named holdouts, with Helios and Golden Instinct as the standard answer. One-time quest props like the Arcane Machine are fine now that quests replay.
