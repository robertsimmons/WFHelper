# CYTE-09 — Reddit farming summary

**Pain level: moderate, but with a solid pity system.** The RNG is genuinely bad; the saving grace is you can just *buy* the blueprints with standing, which is what most people end up doing.

## Prerequisite
Cyte-09 is gated behind **Höllvania / the 1999 content**, which means clearing the story quests to get there. There's no shortcut — the recurring answer to "can I get him early?" is: **pay platinum, or do the quests.** He's not tradeable as a non-Prime frame, and no Prime version exists, so buying blueprints off other players isn't an option either. Ballpark cost quoted for buying him outright: ~£15 / equivalent plat.

## The farm
- Blueprints drop from **Höllvania bounties** and from **Hex-area survival/capture** rotations.
- **The drop rates are rough.** One thread with sourced numbers reported roughly: Survival ~1% Rot A / 2% Rot B / 5% Rot C, Capture well under 2%, and the main blueprint around 3.7%. The prevailing advice at the time: *don't grind bounties past your daily rep cap* — it's not worth the hours.
- **Pity system: buy the parts from Amir** with Hex standing. This is the widely recommended route, and several people said they *prefer* it because it's deterministic. (Note: it's Amir, not Quincy — people get this wrong constantly.)

## Gotchas
- **Bounty mission types rotate**, and the reward attached to each mission type is fixed. So the bounty that drops Systems may simply not be available on a given rotation — you'll see the ability to *buy* Systems before you can ever *farm* it. Not a bug; wait for the rotation or just buy.
- The **Stage Defense** mission (a Cyte-09 reward source) unlocks at a higher Hex rank, when you meet Flare.
- Get the **Prex card** for him in Höllvania — it's near the plant, across from the blue door to the backrooms. If the pickup prompt doesn't appear, leave and re-enter the hub.
- Bring a frame with **loot radar**, or use the **Parallax ship's air support** which locates collectibles — this is the standard advice for the Höllvania collectible hunt generally.

## Community sentiment
Very positive on the frame itself — repeatedly called underrated and S-tier, with build threads showing absurd damage numbers (100M+ and 400M+ builds posted). The consensus is that the acquisition is annoying but the frame is worth it. Nobody describes the grind as *hard*, just low-yield.

**Sources:** r/Warframe threads on Höllvania bounty drop rates, Cyte-09 systems blueprint availability, the Prex location post, and an early-acquisition question thread.
