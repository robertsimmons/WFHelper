# Kullervo — Reddit farming summary

**Pain level: high, and the loudest complaints of the seven.** Not because it's hard — because it's **time-gated by Duviri's mood spiral rotation** and each run is long. The single most repeated piece of advice, by a wide margin: **do it SOLO on NON-Steel-Path.**

## How it works
- Buy all blueprints from **Acrithis in the Dormizone** with **Kullervo's Bane**: 15 for the main BP + 9 each for the three parts = **42 total**.
- Banes require **two things in the same run**: kill Kullervo at **Kullervo's Hold**, *and* then complete the spiral by killing the **Orowyrm**. Kill him and leave early and you get nothing — this trips up a huge number of people.
- Kullervo's Hold **only spawns during Anger, Sorrow, or Fear spirals.**
- Payout: **4/5/6 Banes** per completed run on normal, **6/7/8** on Steel Path.

## The big consensus: solo, normal, Duviri Experience
- **Duviri Experience, not Lone Story.** In Lone Story, Kullervo only appears if he's randomly selected as an objective — you can waste a whole run. In Duviri Experience, as long as the Hold is present in the current spiral he's always available as a **side objective**: grab a few decrees, go to the Hold, talk to **The Warden**, fight him, then finish the Orowyrm. (Caveat: the Warden won't appear if Kullervo *was* selected as the main objective — in that case you just do it as the main objective.)
- **Steel Path is a trap.** Near-unanimous. The extra Banes (roughly +2) don't come close to offsetting how much longer and more annoying SP is. The OP of the biggest complaint thread later posted an update conceding the solo-normal advice was right.
- **Reported times solo non-SP: ~13–17 minutes per run**, sometimes 25–30 with bad objective rolls. That's roughly 8–10 runs for the full 42.
- Take **8–10 decrees** before fighting him, then nuke him. With maxed intrinsics some people skip decrees entirely.
- If you have a strong **Amp + arcanes and melee**, you can fight him with no decrees at all.

## Why public lobbies are the actual problem
Every complaint traced back to randoms: squads farming decrees for 40+ minutes before doing objectives, squads refusing to go to the Undercroft, and squads where nobody knows the Orowyrm mechanics (one thread describes a 50-minute run that ended with everyone quitting).

## Steel Path Orowyrm mechanics (if you insist)
- There's an extra **ring-alignment puzzle** before you can transfer into the Orowyrm — all the pathos valve arrows must line up. It highlights which wheel you're on; two people fiddling with it at once will just undo each other.
- The boss can force you onto a **Kaithe to kill Wyrmlings** — but this is **optional**: you can walk outside the ring of fire and shoot them normally, or wall-latch and kill from there.

## Orowyrm traversal tips (worth reading even on normal)
- You **jump off and re-grapple** repeatedly to move up the Wyrm — it's segmented with grapple points all the way to the head. There's no traversal animation, so it feels broken if you don't know.
- The **energy ring** it periodically sends down will knock you off; it's on a roughly 30-second, very telegraphed cycle. Hop off and re-grapple to dodge it.
- Chasing it on the Kaithe: **destroy the orbs it throws with the Orvius** — they generate currents that give you a speed boost. Alternatively, hold sprint, aim slightly downward to build falling speed, then press jump mid-dive to convert it into forward speed; stack this and you outpace the Wyrm easily.
- In a group, one person grabs the Orvius while another flies ahead — solo you can't do both, hence the speed tricks.

## Undercroft mission types
Solo, **Alchemy** and **Void Flood** are the fastest (2–3 min each). **Defense / Survival / Excavation** are the time sinks. Alchemy is fine solo but the progress bar crawls with 3–4 players.

## The resource grind (the part people forget)
Kullervo also needs **~300 plants** (Dracroots, Yao Shrubs, Maw Fangs). Multiple people called this worse than the Banes. **The trick:** grab the resource-doubling decree, harvest everything, then complete **one decree/objective** to force a save — you can then **abort/extract and keep the plants** without finishing the Orowyrm.

## Alternative for the Pathos Clamp half
**Isleweaver** gives ~15 Pathos Clamps per run on SP in about 5 minutes — good for clamps, but **it does not spawn Kullervo**. Useful if you're multi-farming; useless for Banes.

## Community sentiment
Genuinely split. A vocal group loves Duviri and found this a relaxed multi-farm. A larger group finds it miserable and several people admitted they **just bought him with plat and called it their best plat purchase**. Notable recurring point: **a Kullervo Prime is expected**, and several people are deliberately waiting rather than farming a second copy to subsume. His **Wrathful Advance** subsume is the single most cited reason people farm him at all.

**Sources:** four r/Warframe Kullervo threads including a large recent complaint thread (~3mo) with extensive tips in the comments.
