# Helios — Reddit farming summary

**Pain level: low, but daily-capped.** No RNG at all — Helios is a **straight standing purchase from Cephalon Simaris** in the Sanctuary (Relays). The entire "grind" is farming Simaris rep, which is capped daily. So this is a *calendar* problem, not a difficulty problem.

## The consensus method: stealth scanning in Steel Path open worlds

This came up in essentially every thread, from multiple independent people:

1. Go to **Steel Path Orb Vallis** or **Steel Path Plains of Eidolon** (SP enemies are higher level = far more standing per scan).
2. Get in your **Archwing and fly ~130–150m up**. Above ~100m enemies can't detect you, so every scan counts as a **stealth scan**. Watch out for anti-air emplacements, which seem to spot you slightly higher.
3. Turn on **enemy highlighting in Accessibility options** — repeatedly called out as the single biggest QoL improvement, since you're scanning red dots from very far away.
4. Scan a dense camp, fly far enough away to despawn them, come back, repeat.

**Reported times: 3–10 minutes to cap daily standing.** The Orb Vallis **Temple of Profit** was the most-recommended specific spot (fly to the anti-air emplacement on the east road, then back, to reset spawns). On Plains, people fly between two or three nearby camps.

## The cap-bypass trick (important)
Simaris's **daily Synthesis Target** gives ~5k–10k standing on turn-in that **does NOT count against your daily cap**. Order matters:
- Do the target scans first (each SP target scan is worth ~6–7k, and *that* part does count toward cap),
- then turn it in for the bonus that bypasses the cap,
- then top off with stealth scanning.

Several people said doing this alone can nearly cap you at lower MR.

## Alternatives people prefer
- **Ivara / Loki / Octavia invisibility** on a regular mission instead of Archwing — slower but no aiming required. Popular picks: SP Kuva Fortress Exterminate or Spy, SP Adaro (Sedna).
  - The Exterminate trick: prowl straight to extraction, the Grineer AI paths everyone there, scan the crowd unalerted, then wipe them with a **silenced weapon (Hush mod)** so the next wave also spawns unalerted.
  - Spy variant: **security cameras are scannable** for ~130 standing each and add up fast.
- **Level cap runs** — if you already do them, a handful of scans instantly caps you (~6k per scan). Not worth doing *for* Simaris.
- **Equinox Rest / Rage subsume** to sleep enemies before scanning.

## Gotchas
- **Alerted enemies give reduced standing** — back off and let them settle if you get spotted.
- **Do NOT use Shade's Ghost or Huras Kubrow's Cloak** — the invisibility breaks when you scan. Ivara's Prowl is fine.
- Don't put Assault Mode on a sentinel, or use Elusive Posture on beast claws, or your pet will alert everything.
- **Buy the two Simaris widgets first** (Sol-Battery-style scanner widget + the double-scan chance one) — they make everything meaningfully faster and also help with Kavat genetic codes.
- You **can't use the gear wheel in Duviri**, so no scanning there.

## Worth noting
Several people said Simaris standing stays worth capping long after you've bought everything: **Energy/Health Conversion and Companion Rivens sell well** (unrolled Verglas rivens reportedly a few hundred plat; sentinel-specific mods like Botanist ~45–50p).

**Sources:** four r/Warframe Simaris standing threads (3mo, 6mo, ~1y old), heavy agreement across all of them.
