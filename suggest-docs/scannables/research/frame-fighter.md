# Frame Fighter Data

Blue floating orbs hidden in star chart missions, one per Warframe. One scan unlocks that frame in the Frame Fighter minigame, and scanning every one awards the Frame Fighter Poster decoration.

**Pain level: high, pure RNG per run.** Each planet holds a pool of 2 to 3 frames and a mission rolls at most one fragment from it, often none, so the real bottleneck is waiting for the specific frame you still need. Jupiter is the worst case: all three of its frames are missing and each run can only give one.

## Gates
- Lua: The Second Dream quest.

## Gear
- Helios with Investigator and a Codex Scanner in the gear wheel (Synthesis Scanner works when no Codex Scanner is left): scans fragments automatically when within range. Near-unanimous in wiki and threads that it saves the hunt. Helios scans give no Simaris standing, which does not matter here.
- Loot Detector, Thief's Wit and Coaction Drift: fragments show on the minimap with their own mark, and a bigger loot radar shows them from further away. Several players run these.
- Animal Instinct on a companion widens the same radar.
- Codex or Synthesis Scanner pings: scanner sound gets louder near a fragment. Turn music down, effects up.

## Places
Every planet below hosts its pool across all its star chart missions. Defense, Interception and Archwing missions are skipped because Cephalon-style fragments never spawn there. Capture is the pick everywhere it exists: it ends when the target dies, can't be failed once captured, and the maps are short. One run realistically yields 0 or 1 of the missing items.

### Jupiter: Jupiter star chart missions. Yield 0 or 1 per run, 3 missing.
- Best run: Ananke, Capture, Corpus Gas City. Quick to finish, then roam the gas city at will.
1. Run Ananke and kill the Capture target, then keep exploring while Helios scans.
2. Skip any desaturated orb. It is a frame you already have.

## Gotchas
- Fragments you already scanned still spawn, shown desaturated. Only chase the bright blue ones.
- A planet's pool includes frames you already own, so a bright orb can still be a different frame than the one you need. Scan it anyway, it is progress or a repeat of nothing lost.
- Cephalon Fragments, Somachord Tones and Frame Fighter Data roll independently. A run can hold any mix, or none, and they can stack on the same spot.
- Scan range matters: pass within Helios range or aim a scanner by hand. An orb that is in the room but unscanned counts for nothing.
- Anyone can scan them. The Ludoplex is not needed to unlock the frame in the minigame data, only to play it.
- Void and Kuva Fortress hold none.

## Community sentiment
Players call it a background chore: run Captures or Exterminates with Helios and loot radar until the list empties, and expect to revisit planets with several missing frames. A 2018 thread, still the most-cited location list, matches the wiki's table exactly. Later updates added frames to the same planet pools, which is why older completionists find new orbs on old maps.
