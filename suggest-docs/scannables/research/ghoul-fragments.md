# Ghoul (Grineer Ghoul)

Thirteen Encrypted Journal Fragments about the Grineer Ghouls and Sigor Savah's story; each fills 4 codex scans' worth of lore and unlocks artwork and a transmission.

**Pain level: moderate-high, pure RNG.** Nothing is scanned off a wall. Each fragment is a random drop from killing Ghouls on the Plains of Eidolon or an uncommon stage reward in Ghoul Purge bounties, so the bottleneck is the roughly 49 drops still owed across all 13 entries, with no way to aim at a specific entry.

## Gates
- Vor's Prize (unlocks Cetus and the Plains).
- Mastery Rank 1 for the low Ghoul Purge bounty, Mastery Rank 3 for the high one.

## Gear
- Any frame that kills Ghouls in bulk speeds every route (area damage and crowd control). Common consensus.
- Nekros Desecrate and resource drop chance boosters do not help. The fragments sit on a separate special-loot table (a few players agree, one source stated outright).
- Bring a Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner only if you want to rescan Ghouls for the Codex. Fragments do not need scanning.

## Places
### Plains of Eidolon (Ghoul burial grounds): Cetus, Earth. Yield: about 1 to 2 fragments per run.
- Best run: Cetus, Earth, Ghoul Purge bounty when one is up, otherwise open-world Ghoul hunting on the Plains. The bounty gives a guaranteed stage-reward roll on top of Ghoul kills.
1. Go to Cetus, Earth and take the Plains gate out to Konzu's bounty board.
2. If Ghoul Purge bounties are on the board, take the Mastery Rank 3 one if you can (it has the stronger table) and run all stages. Fragments appear as stage rewards on the second and later stages, roughly 19% of those rolls.
3. Kill every Ghoul you see during the bounty. Each one can drop a fragment.
4. No Ghoul Purge on the board? Walk the Plains and kill Ghouls. They sit buried at Grineer burial grounds and burst out when you get close. Pack leaders (Ghoul Auger Alpha, Ghoul Rictus Alpha) are the same drop.
5. Do not leave the Plains to count progress. A dropped fragment is only credited when you return to Cetus, so a kill-drop and a stage-reward fragment can both show "2/4" but both count once you are back in town.
- Items here: A New Phase, A Tide of Claws and Flesh, Augur, Born To Die, Devourer, Doctor Tengus, Ghouls, Overwhelming Numbers, Rictus, Shock Troops, Strength In Numbers, The Expired, Unstoppable.

## Gotchas
- These are not scannable objects. The Codex Scanner finds nothing; the only source is a drop.
- Ghoul Purge is a recurring event that raises Ghoul numbers and adds two bounties; between events the Plains still spawn Ghouls but fewer.
- Fragment drops from kills count when you are back in Cetus. Dying or aborting before returning can lose them.
- Once an entry is complete players report its fragment stops dropping, so late runs only roll the entries you still need.
- Ghouls in the Olympus Disruption on Mars are not a fragment source; the Plains are.

## Community sentiment
Launch-era players called the farm far too grindy when fragments were bounty-only (a nine-hour stream farm finished under a third of them). Letting Ghouls drop fragments in the open Plains was widely praised because you can pick them up casually while mining or fishing. Consensus is to farm them passively on the Plains rather than chase Purge runs.
