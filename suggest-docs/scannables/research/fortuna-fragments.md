# Fortuna Mem-Fragments (Orb Vallis Solaris)

Thirty-five fixed Debt-Bond-shaped scannables on the Orb Vallis, five per Solaris character (Eudico, Legs, Little Duck, Rude Zuud, Smokefinger, The Business, Ticker), each one unlocking a Codex lore entry.

**Pain level: tedious, not hard.** Nothing random gates them: every fragment sits at a fixed spot, but there is no in-game marker and the Vallis is huge, so the bottleneck is finding 34 small objects in caves, ruins and on peaks. One sweep with a Helios, or a Codex Scanner and patience, finds roughly half.

## Gates
- Orb Vallis access (the Venus Fortuna node). No quest gates the fragments themselves.

## Gear
- Helios or Helios Prime with the Investigator precept and a Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner in the gear wheel: it scans fragments before you see them. Several players say it finds ones they could not spot, near-unanimous among threads that mention it. Investigator skips completed entries, so it never wastes charges on ones you have.
- A Synthesis Scanner with the Sol-Battery widget makes Investigator scans free of charges.
- Do not scan alongside your own Helios on the same fragment: simultaneous scans can double-count and glitch that fragment's progress.

## Places
### Orb Vallis: the Fortuna elevator or the Orb Vallis node on Venus. Yield 15 to 34 per run.
- Best run: Orb Vallis free-roam on Venus, solo, no timer. Roughly 21 fragments are out in the open, the rest are inside caves and structures.
1. Take the Fortuna elevator (or load the Orb Vallis node) with a Codex Scanner and Helios equipped. Sweep the peaks, ruins and odd-looking objects first; outdoor ones sit near peaks and structures.
2. Check every cave. Fragment caves are usually the ones near where Toroids spawn. A shallow dent in a mountain counts as a cave, so look in every pocket of a cave cluster.
3. Scan each Debt-Bond with the Codex Scanner until the scan finishes. Which one of a character's five you grab does not matter: they always unlock in order, so any Eudico fragment gives the next missing Eudico entry.
4. Fragments already scanned stay in the world, desaturated. Skip those; nothing new comes from rescanning.
- Items here: all 34 missing entries below.

## Gotchas
- The five per character unlock in order whichever you scan, so the 1/5 to 5/5 numbering on the card is a count, not a map location. A fresh Legs fragment fills the next missing Legs entry (the one already done is 2/5).
- Scanning with your own Helios at the same moment as your scanner can double-count and glitch that fragment's progress; let one of them do it.
- Fragments are static map objects, not per-mission spawns, so every sweep shows the same set. Ones you have already done appear desaturated and give nothing.
- Fragments in caves are near the toroid-spawn caves; do not skip a cave because it looks empty from the entrance, since some are shallow dents with a hidden pocket.
- Somachord tones and Frame Fighter data are different scannables that also appear on the Vallis, and only one of each can show up at a time. They are separate codex groups, not these.

## Community sentiment
- Players called it a long, fiddly hunt rather than a hard one: one mapper spent about ten hours on the first full find and the community then made a shared map to finish it.
- Several players ask for an in-game marker for found fragments; there is none, so tracking which you have is on you.
- No reward for finishing the set beyond the lore entries, near-unanimous; it is worth it only for Codex completion.
