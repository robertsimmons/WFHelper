# Prex cards (frame lore cards)

Tarot-style Warframe art cards hidden as pickups in hubs, relays and missions; each one picked up completes its Codex Prex entry and becomes an Orbiter decoration.

**Pain level: trivial to low.** Every card is one interact on a fixed spot, so there is no RNG and no scanner. The real bottleneck is gates: the 11 Leverian cards stay invisible until that frame is Rank 30, and four cards sit behind late quests.

## Gates
- Leverian cards (Ash, Atlas, Dante, Gauss, Grendel, Ivara, Lavos, Nezha, Nova, Styanax, Voruna): that frame, or its Prime, at Rank 30.
- Caliban Prex: Caliban at Rank 30, and The New War (unlocks Drifter's Camp).
- Jade Prex: Jade at Rank 30, and Jade Shadows (unlocks Ascension).
- Gyre Prex: Angels of the Zariman.
- Nokko Prex: The New War (unlocks The Airlock in Fortuna).
- Cyte-09 and Temple Prex: The Hex (unlocks Höllvania Central Mall).
- Oraxia Prex: The Hex (unlocks Isleweaver).
- Uriel Prex: The Old Peace (unlocks The Descendia).
- Dagath Prex: a clan dojo with the Dagath's Hollow room built.

## Places
### Leverian galleries: Codex or Market, no mission. Yield 11 per sitting.
- Best run: sit in the Orbiter and open each gallery from the Codex; no load into a mission, solo, and every Rank 30 frame's card is one gallery away.
1. Open the Codex, pick the Warframe, press the Leverian button (lower right). Or open the Leverian tab of the Codex.
2. Walk around in first person to the spot below and press interact on the card. It only shows if that frame (or its Prime) is Rank 30.
3. Do the galleries in one go, then leave; each card lands in your inventory when picked up.
- Items here: Ash Prex, Atlas Prex, Dante Prex, Gauss Prex, Grendel Prex, Ivara Prex, Lavos Prex, Nezha Prex, Nova Prex, Styanax Prex, Voruna Prex.

### Kronia Relay: Saturn. Yield 5 per visit.
- Best run: Kronia Relay itself; it is a hub, no mission, solo, and all five rooms are within a short walk.
1. Fly to Saturn and dock at Kronia Relay.
2. Visit the five spots: Protea behind the pillar right of the rightmost Ducat station; Titania on top of the Earth projection in New Loka's room; Garuda on the ground behind the tombstone left of Jade's Promise, back right of Teshin's room; Vauban on top of barrels up and left of the entrance to Steel Meridian's room; Ember on the archway above the Grineer table behind the Red Veil flag in Red Veil's room.
3. These do not need the frame at Rank 30. They show a Forma icon when picked up.
- Items here: Ember Prex, Garuda Prex, Protea Prex, Titania Prex, Vauban Prex.

### Höllvania Central Mall: Orbiter Pom-2 computer. Yield 2 per visit.
- Best run: the mall hub itself, no mission; both cards are a minute apart.
1. Use the Pom-2 computer in the Orbiter to go to the mall.
2. Cyte-09: second floor, inside the tall planter opposite the escalators when you exit the Backroom (the clothing shop entry).
3. Temple: enter the Round Table Pub, face the crowd from the stage where Flare stands, find the small table by the brick half-wall, then follow the half-wall to the next small table. The card is on the floor by a leg.
- Items here: Cyte-09 Prex, Temple Prex.

### Drifter's Camp: Earth. Yield 1.
- Best run: switch base to the camp and look around; no mission.
1. Go to Earth on the Star Chart and select the Drifter's Camp node.
2. Cross the partly hidden bridge at ground level to the right of Kahl's rocky outcrop (seen from the top of the crashed lander), into the area past the small waterfall.
3. The card is on a rock formation across the river next to a cave, behind a pair of destructible rocks. Caliban must be Rank 30 or it does not appear.
- Items here: Caliban Prex.

### Chrysalith: Zariman Ten Zero. Yield 1.
- Best run: the Chrysalith hub; no mission.
1. Enter the Zariman and stay in the Chrysalith hub.
2. Walk the hallway after the docking bays to Cephalon Melica. The card is on top of the lockers across from her.
- Items here: Gyre Prex.

### Clan dojo: Dagath's Hollow room. Yield 1.
- Best run: a solo clan dojo; no mission.
1. Build the Dagath's Hollow room if the dojo lacks it. Its research is already done, so it can be built immediately.
2. Walk in through the large entrance, look straight up to the hanging fire pot, and bullet-jump to the ledge on the column just right of it.
3. The card is on the ledge.
- Items here: Dagath Prex.

### Fortuna: Venus. Yield 1.
- Best run: the Fortuna hub; no mission.
1. Go to The Airlock in Fortuna (needs The New War).
2. Climb to the upper balcony. The card is behind large crates.
- Items here: Nokko Prex.

### Ascension: Brutus, Uranus. Yield 1.
- Best run: Ascension on Brutus; the card sits just south of the start, so grab it first. Jade must be Rank 30 or the pickup prompt does not show.
1. Start the mission and head directly south of the starting area.
2. Look under the ramp: from the first circular platform go forward, drop to the left/right bridge, drop onto the ramp under it, walk a few metres up, turn right and drop over the edge. The card is on the rock in front of you.
3. Picking it up puts it straight in your inventory.
- Items here: Jade Prex.

### Isleweaver: Duviri. Yield 1.
- Best run: Isleweaver; a long mission but the card is on an open island, so you can fly or run to it first.
1. Start Isleweaver (Duviri).
2. Go to the large lone island southwest of Castle Town. The card is on the undercliff at the bottom left.
- Items here: Oraxia Prex.

### The Descendia: Dark Refractory. Yield 1.
- Best run: the Descendia solo to floor 21; the card is the first thing at the bottom of floor 21 and checkpoints at floors 7 and 14 let you stop and return.
1. Open the Descendia from the Dark Refractory in the Navigation console.
2. Clear the 21 Infernums in order; the 7th, 14th and 21st are checkpoints.
3. On floor 21, as you drop in, turn behind you. The card is on the floor in front of the chained statue by the pyre, opposite the door. Only one squad member can pick it up per instance, so go solo or pick it first.
- Items here: Uriel Prex.

## Gotchas
- A Prex is picked up with the interact key, not scanned. Scanners, Helios Investigator, Loot Detector and Thief's Wit do nothing here.
- Leverian cards, Caliban's and Jade's do not render until the frame is Rank 30. If you stand on the spot and see nothing, check the rank.
- Kronia Relay cards sit in corners that are easy to miss; look up and behind pillars and flags.
- Cards that are bought or quest-given (Sevagoth, Yareli, Citrine, Koumei, Follie, Narin, Heart of Deimos, Heirloom, Tennotober, Sirius and Orion) do not count toward the Codex Prex section. Do not chase them for this group.

## Community sentiment
Players treat these as hidden-object puzzles and mostly post location guides when new ones drop. The agreed answer to "is there a card for every frame" is no, but every Leverian frame has one, and Kronia Relay holds the biggest cluster. The relay cards are the favourite complaint for being hard to spot.
