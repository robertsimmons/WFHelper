# Mesa Prime — Reddit farming summary

**Pain level: moderate, and it's a currency grind, not a mission grind.**

> ⚠️ **Verify first:** Mesa Prime is vaulted, so the below assumes she's not currently part of a rotating unvaulting. If she's been unvaulted, her relics drop normally from missions and you can ignore the Aya route entirely. Check the current Prime Resurgence rotation before committing.

## The core fact people repeat constantly
**Resurgence relics do not drop anywhere.** You cannot farm Mesa Prime's relics from missions while she's vaulted. You farm **Aya** and buy the relics from **Varzia** in Maroo's Bazaar. There is no alternative besides trading for the parts directly with other players.

Small footnote: Aya *can* drop from regular Void missions, but it's uncommon and nobody recommends it as a primary route.

## Aya farming — the two camps

### Cetus (the min-max pick)
The optimized method, and the one with an actual dedicated community around it:
- Run **tier 5 (level 40–60) bounties**. **Search and Rescue** and **Find the Hidden Artifact** are the fastest because they have the fewest stages that force you to sit and wait.
- **Take a bounty from Konzu first, then deliberately fail stage 1** — this locks all plains camps to the level 40–60 tier. Then re-take bounties from the **grineer tents scattered around the plains** rather than returning to Cetus.
- **Bring Volt or Nova.** This is not optional for speed:
  - **Volt's Speed** buffs the escort **drone** and the rescue target.
  - **Nova's portals teleport the drone** — walk in front of it and place a portal and it voops through. Don't place the exit too high off the ground or the drone won't take it, and a player has to be near the drone for it to path to a portal.
- With this, bounties run **3–5 minutes**. Reported yield: **~20 Aya/hour**, with one screenshot post showing a very large haul from ~2 hours.
- Caveat: some people report the tents not offering Search and Rescue at tier 5, so there's some inconsistency here.

### Deimos (the relaxed pick)
Slightly worse Aya rate per bounty overall, but several experienced players argue it's the better *total* return:
- Bounties are shorter tilesets, easier, and work with almost any frame.
- **Mother Tokens are uncapped** (unlike Cetus standing), and you can spend tokens on **relic packs**, which loop back into more Aya.
- You also passively pick up **Endo, Ayatan stars, and Kuva**.
- Downside: **Loid still gets stuck** on iso vaults — bring Nova.

**Why Cetus wins on paper but not always in practice:** Deimos has a better rate on the *final* stage, but Cetus is higher across the whole bounty. Cetus's problems are practical — the drone gets stuck on rocks constantly, and the cave exterminates are miserable.

**Orb Vallis** gets occasional mentions as a fun-but-suboptimal third option; a few people swear their personal luck is better there.

## Context worth knowing
- The **Cetus Aya farm was nerfed** (bounty-rerolling exploit removed), which is why older guides overstate the rates.
- Aya has real trade value: people quoted **5–8 plat per Aya**, commonly traded as **6 Aya : 36p**. So over-farming Aya is a legitimate plat farm, not wasted effort.
