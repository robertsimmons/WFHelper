# Cephalon fragments (classic, one per planet/topic)

Blue floating data blocks hidden in missions on each planet; every entry is a Codex lore page with Ordis narration, and scanning it enough times completes the entry.

**Pain level: moderate, tedious.** One mission shows at most one Cephalon fragment, and which of that planet's entries it shows is out of your hands, so every entry is a run count on its planet: roughly two runs per scan, 3 to 7 scans per entry. The real bottleneck is spotting a small block in a big map; a loot radar mod turns it from a hunt into a glance at the minimap.

## Gates
- Lua: The Second Dream (quest).

## Gear
- Thief's Wit (Exilus mod on the frame): near-unanimous. Shows the fragment on the minimap as a blue quadruple diamond; rank it to 5 for +42 m.
- Animal Instinct (companion mod): common. Stacks with Thief's Wit.
- Loot Detector (aura) and Coaction Drift: wiki tips, common. More radar range.
- Helios with Investigator: wiki tip, some. Scans fragments in range for you. Needs a Codex or Synthesis Scanner equipped in the gear wheel for scan charges, and never scans a finished entry.
- Codex Scanner: required (or Synthesis Scanner). 25 charges cost 500 credits from the Market Gear section; a fragment scan uses one charge. Synthesis charges cost ten times more and its Cross-Matrix Widget does not double fragment scans, so use the Codex Scanner.

## Places
All places work the same way: the fragment is a random spawn point somewhere in the map, each run on that planet is one roll, and the planet's mission list is the only variable. Run the node, find the fragment, scan it with the equipped scanner, finish the mission. Yield is 0 or 1 scan per run for every place below, about 1 run in 2 shows one.

### Mercury: Grineer Asteroid and Grineer Galleon. Yield 0 to 1 per run.
- Best run: Elion (Capture, Grineer Asteroid); M Prime (Exterminate) as the back-up. Short and lets you walk the whole map.
1. Run Elion solo with Thief's Wit on. Check the minimap for the blue diamond while you move toward the Capture target.
2. Scan the fragment, then finish the mission. A fragment scan only counts if the mission ends.
- Items here: Mercury, Grineer Leadership, Archaic Weapons.

### Venus: Corpus Outpost and Corpus Ship. Yield 0 to 1 per run.
- Best run: Venera (Capture, Corpus Outpost); E Gate (Exterminate) as the back-up.
1. Run Venera solo with a loot radar and watch the minimap while heading to the target.
2. Scan, then finish the mission.
- Items here: Venus, Dojo, Corpus Crew.

### Mars: Grineer Settlement, Corpus Ship, Corpus Ice Planet. Yield 0 to 1 per run.
- Best run: Ara (Capture, Grineer Settlement); Hellas (Exterminate) as the back-up. Mars maps have a lot of vertical drop, so give each tile a full look.
1. Run Ara with Thief's Wit; after the target dies, if no fragment showed, check the minimap again while bullet-jumping up. The radar only reaches 12 m up and down.
2. Scan, then finish the mission.
- Items here: Mars, Mars Settlement, Sentinels.

## Gotchas
- The mission must finish for the scan to count. A failed or abandoned mission loses the scan.
- At most one Cephalon fragment per mission. Defense, Interception and Archwing missions never spawn one.
- A fragment of an entry you already finished still spawns and cannot be scanned. A marker you have finished or already scanned this mission leaves the minimap.
- Each fragment type rolls separately, about 50% per mission (common). A Somachord tone (purple arc) or Frame Fighter data may show instead or alongside; ignore them and scan the blue block.
- Cephalon fragments share spawn points with Syndicate Medallions. If a map shows a medallion but no fragment, the fragment did not roll.
- Mars shows a fragment 100% of the time only while a Junction needs those entries from you. Otherwise it is the same roll as everywhere.
- The radar is a cylinder, 12 m up and down (some). In tall tiles, bullet-jump up and watch the minimap. Fragments have turned up under extraction pads.
- Limbo cannot scan while in the Rift. Equipped scanner holsters your guns until you swap back.
- The loot radar is not shared in squads; run solo.
- The scanner takes about 1 second of holding fire on the block. A Codex Scanner with 0 charges does nothing, so carry a full set.

## Community sentiment
Players call the hunt miserable without a loot radar (one reported two hours on Mars for a single fragment) and trivial with Thief's Wit, after which they trip over fragments while doing other things. The advice in every thread is the same: equip a loot radar, run quick Capture or Exterminate missions, search each tile, and repeat.
