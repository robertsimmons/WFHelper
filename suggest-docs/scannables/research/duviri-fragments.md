# Duviri fragments

Collectible audio drives in Duviri: ten Lost Islands of Duviri codex entries narrated by Acrithis, plus one Isleweaver entry narrated by Major Neci Rusalka. Completing them fills the Fragments codex.

**Pain level: tedious but short, no RNG.** Every pickup sits at a fixed spot on a static map, but the Lost Islands entries need 90 pickups across the whole overworld with no in-game tracker, and the Isleweaver entry needs 15 more inside one mission. The bottleneck is knowing where each one is, so keep the wiki map open.

## Gates
- The Duviri Paradox quest (opens Duviri).
- The Hex quest (opens the Isleweaver node).

## Gear
- Listen for the looping fragment sound, the wiki's own method for locating a fragment (the wiki's stated method, no Reddit weight).
- Warframe and Drifter pickups are the same interact prompt; no scanner, companion or Helios helps, and companions and gear items are not allowed in Duviri.

## Places
### Duviri overworld: the Duviri node on the Star Chart (Dominus Thrax bust, upper right) or the Duviri door in the Dormizone. Yield: up to all 10 Lost Islands entries over a few runs.
- Best run: The Duviri Experience, solo. It keeps the full overworld open, and the Lone Story strips the side content you wander through. Circuit never leaves the Undercroft, so no fragments there.
1. Open the wiki's Duviri map at https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Duviri/Map and turn on the Fragments layers. Each of the ten entries has its own marker colour, nine markers each.
2. Start the run, ride out of Teshin's Cave, and sweep islands entry by entry. Follow the fragment sound and press interact on each drive.
3. Go into the caves. Several are inside them: the console on the north of the Caves of Academe island opens a floor hatch; the Island of Lorn has several inside a cave; some Doll Mausoleum and Scholar's Landing drives are behind brush you must crouch or slide through.
4. Take a Decree before you leave. Duviri progress, fragments included, is saved when you earn one; or extract through the portal after the Orowyrm.
- Items here: Lake Verula, Manipura Island, Scholar’s Landing, The Bleeding Earth, The Caves of Academe, The Doll Mausoleum, The Galleria, The Island of Lorn, Watcher’s Island, We Are Not What We Were.

### Isleweaver: the Isleweaver node in Duviri. Yield: Along Came A Spider in one run.
- Best run: Isleweaver, solo. All 15 fragments can be collected in a single run (confirmed by two players, a squad run is fine too).
1. Pick Isleweaver from the Duviri node list in Teshin's Cave (Dominus Thrax stands there). Only Castle Town and Scholar's Landing are explorable.
2. Sweep Teshin's Cave first: one fragment sits there.
3. Explore Castle Town and Scholar's Landing for the rest, following the fragment sound.
4. When the mission sends you to the Undercroft, look around the transition map during the 10 to 15 seconds you get; several fragments hide there and players call it the worst part.
5. Finish the mission after all 15. An inbox message with Rusalka's Hat and Medals Mementos arrives.
- Items here: Along Came A Spider.

## Gotchas
- The tracker shows 1 per entry; the wiki's 9 (and 15) pickups are what actually completes each one.
- Fragments are pickups from the world, not codex scans, so the scanner and Helios do nothing.
- Fragment progress in Duviri is saved at a Decree; quitting before one can lose the run's pickups.
- The Isleweaver node walls you off from the rest of Duviri; the Lost Islands entries cannot be collected there.
- Isleweaver ran as Operation: Eight Claw but the node stayed as a game mode as of Update 42.

## Community sentiment
Players treat the Lost Islands as a long wiki-map chore and the Isleweaver set as the annoying one because of the Undercroft hiding spots. Completionists list both with the open-world fragments and say nothing about RNG, only time. The lore is widely loved.
