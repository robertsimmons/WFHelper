# Albrecht (Entrati)

Five lore fragments, Albrecht's Notes, collected as 23 pages in total; finishing them fills the Albrecht's Notes codex entries.

**Pain level: grind, but low risk.** Every page comes from killing a Mocking or Scathing Whisper, and each kill only has a 50% chance to leave a page. The bottleneck is that a Whisper is a random spawn, not a guaranteed one, and pages arrive strictly in entry order, so no entry can be finished early.

## Gates
Whispers in the Walls (quest)
The Deadlock Protocol (quest), only to use the Armatus Disruption node

## Gear
Void damage and an Amp (wiki recommendation): both deal heavy damage to the Whisper's Grimoire, which is the part that must die.
Nothing in the game speeds up finding a Whisper. Scanners, Helios and loot gear do nothing here.

## Places
### Albrecht's Laboratories: Deimos nodes Nex, Persto, Cambire, Munio, Effervo and Armatus, all entered from the Sanctum Anatomica. Yield about 1 page per Exterminate run.
- Best run: Nex, Deimos, Exterminate. It is short, ends the moment the kills are done, and lets you sweep every room for a Whisper on your own pace. Exterminate runs are also where players report finding Whispers most often.
1. Finish Whispers in the Walls, then ride the Sanctum Anatomica elevator to Nex and start Exterminate.
2. Walk every room, dead ends included. Tagfer says a line when a Whisper Grimoire is near; a floating book is the thing you are looking for.
3. Interact with the floating Grimoire to unbind it. The Whisper appears, a Mocking Whisper is melee and a Scathing Whisper is ranged.
4. Empty its first health bar, then find the white-marked Whisper Grimoire it spawns elsewhere in the tileset and destroy it. The step people get wrong: if you take too long to destroy the Grimoire, the Whisper recovers its health.
5. When the book is banished, a page may drop (50%). It carries a waypoint marker; grab it. If none dropped, start the next run.
6. Optional faster loop: take a Sanctum Anatomica bounty from Fibonacci and look for "Deadly Whispers". Its objective is to defeat a Whisper, so it spawns for certain and shows a waypoint. Not every bounty offers it, and bounties refresh on a timer, so check the list when you are there.
- Items here: Albrecht's Notes - The Aftermath, Albrecht's Notes - The Cavia, Albrecht's Notes - Duviri, Albrecht's Notes - The Vessels, Albrecht's Notes - We End as we Began.

## Gotchas
- Pages always drop in entry order, so killing Whispers never helps a later entry until the earlier ones are full.
- You must destroy the Grimoire, not only drain the Whisper; the page comes when the book is banished.
- Whispers still spawn after you hold some pages, and still spawn after all are collected. They are a random spawn, so some runs have none; a bounty with "Deadly Whispers" is the only guaranteed one.
- Alchemy and Mirror Defense runs are reported to be poor for finding Whispers by one player; the rest of the hunt happened in Exterminate and Assassination.
- Public squads make it harder, since teammates may clear the tileset or leave before you reach the Grimoire.

## Community sentiment
Players call the notes an easy, obscure collection that most missed at launch. The consistent advice is to run Exterminate, check every room, and use the Deadly Whispers bounty when it appears. One group reported 35 Whispers across 25 Exterminate runs, and others report long dry spells.
