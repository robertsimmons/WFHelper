# Hound — Reddit farming summary

**Pain level: moderate-to-high, but mostly avoidable.** The strongest recurring advice: **most people don't need to build one at all.**

## Two ways to get a Hound
1. **Just take the ones you're given.** Every **vanquished** Sister of Parvos mails you a **fully-built Hound**. Several people said they've never built one — they just farm Sisters and keep the ones whose part combination they like. Since the parts are cosmetic-ish (see below), this is usually enough.
2. **Build a custom one** from part blueprints, which drop from the Sister confrontation. This is the RNG-heavy path.

**Critical: you only get the Hound and the parts if you VANQUISH the Sister.** Convert her and you get nothing. Several confused threads trace back to this.

## Drop reality
- Part drops are **pure luck** — no way to target a specific part.
- **Do the final confrontation in a full squad.** You get an extra part from each other player's Sister, which is the single biggest rate multiplier available.
- That said, multiple people report getting **only ~1 part across 7–12 Sisters** even in groups, so the "each Sister drops a part" claim may not hold or may be bugged. Set expectations accordingly.
- **You can trade the blueprints** with other players — repeatedly recommended as the sane option if you don't want to grind Sisters.

## Does the part combination actually matter?
Mostly **no**. The consensus:
- The only real stat consideration is getting **at least 343 base shields** so **Reinforced Bond** procs (relevant if you're running Calculated Redirection / Link Redirection, e.g. on Hildryn).
- Beyond that it's **appearance**, plus you need to build/claim one of each part **once** for mastery and to unlock the mods.
- Weapons and mods matter far more than the chassis combo.

## Farming Sisters efficiently (the actual bottleneck)
From a detailed how-to post, if you don't care about the Tenet weapon:
- Go **solo, non-Steel-Path Capture on Pluto (Hydra)**. Playing with a team **raises the required kill count**, making it harder.
- Equip the **Xoris** — heavy-attack/detonate on the Granum ghosts to charge it, then detonate on the captured Solaris target for a big time extension in the Granum Void.
- Find a **gold hand**, waypoint it, kill the capture target, then return and insert the **Zenith crown**. Kill 25 Parvos units inside; the triangles they drop add time.
- **Stay in one area** — there's roughly a 20-enemy spawn cap, and new ones won't appear until you clear the current batch. A chaining laser weapon (Larkspur + a Dig dog for infinite archgun ammo) or a nuke frame works well.
- **If the gold hand is within ~200m of extraction the Sister may not spawn.** If you haven't found a hand halfway through the tile, just restart.
- Kill the **Treasurer** for a new Zenith crown each cycle.
- **Progenitor element no longer matters much** — you can buy a **valence override from Eleanor at the Hex** (~20k standing) to set the element after 4–5 forma.
- Weapons are on a **knockout system**: down the Sister, look at the weapon, and if you don't want it just extract without holding interact — that cycles it out of your pool. You can pre-cycle before a new Tenet weapon releases so you get it in 1–2 runs on launch day.
- Hit **Replay** on the white end screen to instantly re-run.

## Community sentiment
Hounds are well-regarded — one comment called them "downright broken sometimes" — and they're the companion people point to when arguing against MOAs. But almost nobody enjoys Sister hunting for its own sake; it's tolerated as a means to Tenet weapons, with Hounds as a byproduct.

**Sources:** r/Warframe threads on Hound part farming, Sister of Parvos Hound part drops, and a detailed "How to get a Sister of Parvos" instructional post.
