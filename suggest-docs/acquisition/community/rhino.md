# Rhino — Reddit farming summary

**Pain level: very low.** Universally treated as one of the two or three easiest frames in the game to get. Nobody complains about this grind.

## How it works
- Component blueprints (Neuroptics / Chassis / Systems) drop from **The Jackal**, the assassination boss on **Fossa, Venus**.
- The **main blueprint is NOT a drop** — buy it from the in-game Market **for credits**. Several threads have people re-running Fossa forever not realizing this.
- Each Jackal kill gives one random Rhino part. Expect roughly 3+ runs, more with bad RNG.

## Tips people actually gave
- **Watch for the two-mission variant.** Multiple people got stuck because Fossa showed as a high-level node — that's either a **Nightmare Mode** variant (daily random) or an **invasion** overlay. Clicking the node gives you both options; pick the plain Assassination version.
- **Jackal has mechanics.** New players report doing zero damage and assuming they're underpowered. You have to break its legs during the stagger phase — worth watching a short video once rather than brute-forcing.
- The node occasionally just bugs out on load; re-queuing fixes it.
- Fossa is on the left side of Venus near the Junction — a couple of threads were literally just "I can't find the node."

## Rhino Prime (adjacent)
Prime version is **vaulted** and does not drop from any mission. Relics come from **Varzia (Prime Resurgence) bought with Aya** — see `mesa-prime.md` for the Aya farming notes, they apply identically here. Worth checking whether he's currently unvaulted before grinding Aya.

## Community sentiment
Mild condescension toward Rhino as a frame — "press 2 buttons every 5 minutes" was one comment, with several people suggesting **Koumei** instead as an early frame that teaches more mechanics. Nobody disputes he's effective, just that he's simple.

**Sources:** r/Warframe threads on farming Rhino parts (2 recent beginner threads), plus an early-frame acquisition list comment that gets reposted a lot.
