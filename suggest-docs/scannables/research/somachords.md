# Somachords

Music tracks you unlock for the Orbiter Somachord by scanning purple-bar Somachord Tones in missions, landscapes and quests. Completing one adds the song to the jukebox and fills its Codex entry.

**Pain level: High.** The bottleneck is spawn luck and hiding spots, not combat. Star chart planets roll a random tone in the tileset and give about one scan per run, most songs need 4 scans, and a scan only saves if you finish the mission. Fixed-spot songs (Duviri, Isleweaver, Perita Rebellion, Albrecht's Laboratories) are easy once you know the room but sit behind quests, puzzles or rotating tiles.

## Gates
- Orb Vallis (roaming tones): Vox Solaris quest.
- Orb Vallis fixed tones (Sunkiller and the two that feed Vallis songs): The New War quest.
- Duviri: The Duviri Paradox quest.
- Isleweaver: The Hex quest.
- Albrecht's Laboratories: Whispers in the Walls quest.
- Zariman: Angels of the Zariman quest.
- Kuva Fortress: The War Within quest.
- Lua Plato node: The Second Dream quest.
- Ascension (Brutus, Uranus): Jade Shadows quest.
- The Perita Rebellion and The Descendia: The Old Peace quest.
- Break Narmer: Veilbreaker quest.
- Granum Void instance: The Deadlock Protocol quest.
- The Awakening track: Awakening quest.

## Gear
- **Loot Radar mod (Loot Detector, Thief's Wit, Animal Instinct or Primed Animal Instinct): near-unanimous.** Tones show as a pink icon on the minimap. Primed Animal Instinct on you and a Kavat or Khora's Venari gives the biggest radius. It does not work while riding a K-Drive, so hop off to check.
- **Golden Instinct (Helminth ability, 15th Metamorphosis): common.** The Void Spark points at the nearest unscanned Somachord within 200m, so you stop memorising spawn points. It ignores tones you already scanned. Narrow Minded to shrink its range makes the spark easier to chase. Lavos's Transmutation Probe shortens its cooldown.
- **Parallax Orokin Eye (landing craft air support): some.** After Golden Instinct points at something, drop the Eye to pin it on the map.
- **Helios with Investigator plus a Codex or Synthesis Scanner equipped: common.** Helios scans tones within 50m at max rank while you run past, so you can't miss a spawn. Helios only uses scan charges and will not scan a tone you already finished.
- **Codex Scanner or Synthesis Scanner in the gear wheel: required.** In Duviri and Break Narmer there is no gear wheel, so you interact with the tone instead.

## Places
### Orb Vallis: Fortuna on Venus, Orb Vallis. Yield 4 per run.
- Best run: Orb Vallis free roam from Fortuna, because a short trick turns every run into one full song. Leave Vallis to save, then re-enter for the next scan set.
1. Walk out onto the Vallis and find one roaming tone. Only one spawns per visit, in one of about fifty spots. Use a Loot Radar mod or Golden Instinct, and get off the K-Drive to check.
2. Scan it. That puts a Vallis song in progress.
3. Go up to the Fortuna entrance roof and scan the tone by the chimneys (the For Narmer tone).
4. Scan the tone on the Spaceport roof behind the cargo launcher (blue beam into the sky; the Hybrid Abominations tone).
5. Scan the Sunkiller tone on the northernmost wall inside the Temple Fabrication. It counts for its own song the first time, then feeds whatever song is in progress after that.
6. Leave and re-enter Vallis before scanning the fixed tones again. Each fixed tone counts once per visit, and it only counts toward a song already started.
- Items here: Cold Tundra, Coldwave, Fluid Mechanics, Klokkit, Null Unit, We All Lift Together, Vapor Trails, Sunkiller.

### Plains of Eidolon: Cetus on Earth. Yield 1 per run.
- Best run: Plains free roam, only needed if you want Sunkiller from here instead of Vallis.
1. Find the Sunkiller tone underneath the Grineer Galleon wreckage north of Gara Toht Lake.
- Items here: Sunkiller.

### Duviri: Duviri. Yield 3 per run.
- Best run: The Lone Story, a Duviri run in the Anger or Fear Spiral, because both Kullervo's Hold and the Amphitheater exist in those moods. The Spiral mood follows the clock in 2-hour blocks, so check the mood before queueing.
1. Pick up Shadows and Summonings in Titan's Rest.
2. Pick up Eyes That Burn behind the Amphitheater.
3. Go to Kullervo's Hold. Rotate the three Dominus Thrax busts on the island to face the centre, hack the console outside the cell cave, and pick up the Unbound tone in the locked cell.
4. Earn another Decree before you leave, or extract after the Orowyrm. Tone progress saves on Decrees.
- Items here: Eyes That Burn, Shadows and Summonings, Unbound.

### Isleweaver: Duviri game mode. Yield 3 per run.
- Best run: Isleweaver, because all three tones are fixed spots.
1. King's Palace, near the fountain on the side.
2. Scholar's Landing, on top of the tower.
3. Fragmented Arena, near the bridge.
- Items here: Beneath Her Eyes, Dust's Dominion, Tethra Jahrak.

### The Perita Rebellion: Dark Refractory on the Navigation console. Yield 1 to 3 per run.
- Best run: Recall (the 12-minute Orders mission), because tones sit in fixed tile rooms and the map is four battlefield tiles long. You see only some tiles per run, so rerun until the rooms below show up.
1. Lullaby of the Manifold: in the tile with many bunkers, in a high cave above a waterfall.
2. Lullaby of the Manifold - Adis's Lament: behind a waterfall in the room with bridges across a bottomless gap.
3. Deepest Dark: in a dead-end Xenoflora shrine off the main path. It is not the corridor tile with two xenoflora patches.
4. Ironclad: in the corridor tile with the chained-up Hunhullus, above its head at the edge of the room.
5. Executor: in a gully at the edge of the canyon tile where you saved the carriage in The Old Peace. It is on a ledge just below the cliff edge.
6. Finish the mission, including the boss, or the scans do not save.
- Items here: Deepest Dark, Ironclad, Executor, Lullaby of the Manifold, Lullaby of the Manifold - Adis's Lament.

### Albrecht's Laboratories: Deimos. Yield 1 to 2 per run.
- Best run: Nex on Deimos (Exterminate), because it walks most of the tileset. The tones sit in specific rooms that do not always roll.
1. The Fragmented: find the room with a computer, a glowing gap, two balconies and a locked door past the gap. Hack the computer on one balcony, use Operator form to activate the reactive crystals just off the ledge of both balconies, then activate the computer you saw at the entrance. The tone is in the unlocked room.
2. Another tone is in a dead-end library room that looks like a church. Interact with the Grimoire Altar there to start a puzzle.
3. Another tone is underground behind the moving wall. This room can take many reruns to appear.
- Items here: No God Above Knowledge, The Fragmented, The Master Returns.

### The Descendia: Dark Refractory on the Navigation console. Yield 1.
- Best run: The Descendia, to Infernum 21. Floors 7 and 14 are checkpoints, so you can leave and resume from them.
1. Clear the floors to Infernum 21.
2. At the entrance of Infernum 21, scan the tone on top of one of the high stone pillars on either side of the room.
- Items here: Roses From The Abyss.

### Ascension: Brutus on Uranus. Yield 2 per run.
- Best run: Ascension from the Brutus node, because the two Jade Shadows tones are in this tileset. Two players found they spawn in the normal star chart run and not in the Operation variant.
1. Run Ascension at Brutus.
2. Look on the floor and up top with a Loot Radar mod or Golden Instinct. Drop the Parallax Orokin Eye air support to pin them.
3. Extract so the scans save.
- Items here: Angels Requiem, Final Ascent.

### Break Narmer: Kahl's weekly missions. Yield 1.
- Best run: Junk Run, which is one of three Break Narmer missions that rotate weekly (Sneaky Sabotage, Junk Run, Prison Break). It is only playable the week it comes up.
1. In the large room with the shock traps for the two Narmer Deacons, take the left path towards the Sprag boss room.
2. Cross to the far end of the boss room and scan the tone next to the left door. Kahl has no gear wheel, so interact with it.
- Items here: Hold The Line.

### Corpus Ship, Phobos tones: Phobos, Pluto, Jupiter and Neptune Corpus Ship nodes. Yield 1 per run.
- Best run: Roche on Phobos (Exterminate).
1. Granum Void: find the Corpus Temple Relief tile, the big pyramid room with a giant coin above it, and scan the tone on top of the coin. Only one Temple Relief tile can spawn per mission and not every run has one.
2. Entwined Forever: kill the Treasurer for a Granum Crown, spend it on a Golden Hand Tribute, then scan the tone on the centre of the ridge in the north-west corner of the Granum Void pocket. The timer is 60 seconds, so do not bring a long search.
- Items here: Granum Void, Entwined Forever.

### Quest replays: the Codex. Yield 1 song per run.
- Best run: replay each quest from the Codex until you have every tone.
1. The Sacrifice (Your Creator 2 scans, To Take Its Pain Away 3 scans): scan the tones in mission order, 5 in total. Reported spots: right as you enter the Courtyard; high up in the corridor in front of Umbra's chamber; far out on scaffolding on Ceres; on a bridge on the side of the room with the five mimics; in a cave to the right after you sync with Umbra. A sixth behaves like a normal roaming tone in the large forest tile before the Ballas fight and counts for the same songs.
2. Call of the Tempestarii (Sleeping In The Cold Below, 2 scans): one is in a spy-vault vent (the O-shaped vault with the laser grids you wait out); the second is at the back of the Tempestarii in the engineering section.
3. The Awakening: nothing to scan. Completing Awakening grants the track, and players who already finished it receive it automatically. If it is still missing, replay Awakening from the Codex once.
- Items here: Your Creator, To Take Its Pain Away, Sleeping In The Cold Below, The Awakening.

### Uranus: Uranus. Yield about 1 per run.
- Best run: Sycorax or Puck (Exterminate, Grineer Sealab), because Exterminate sends you through the whole tileset where the tone can spawn. Ascension at Brutus is a separate place above.
1. Run Exterminate with a Loot Radar mod, Golden Instinct or Helios Investigator active.
2. Scan any tone you see. The scan shows which of the planet's songs it advanced and its progress.
3. Finish the mission to save.
- Items here: Hunhow, Sentient Tombs, Grakata.

### Kuva Fortress: Kuva Fortress. Yield about 1 per run.
- Best run: Dakata (Exterminate), the Fortress's Exterminate node.
1. Run the whole Exterminate through every tile. Tones hide in obscure corners on high or low levels, so a Loot Radar mod's vertical reach is the limit. Fly or climb to different floors.
2. Scan, finish and repeat.
- Items here: Red Broth, The Witches Lair.

### Void: Void. Yield about 1 per run.
- Best run: Teshub (Exterminate, level 10 Orokin Tower), because it is the lowest-level Void node.
1. Run the Exterminate with a Loot Radar mod or Golden Instinct on.
2. Scan any tone and finish the mission.
- Items here: Corrupted, Ghosts of Void.

### Lua: Lua. Yield about 1 per run.
- Best run: Plato (Exterminate) or Copernicus (Capture), both Orokin Moon tileset.
1. Run the Exterminate through the Orokin Moon with a Loot Radar mod or Golden Instinct.
2. Scan and finish.
- Items here: Rapid Adaptation, The Second Dream.

### Zariman: Zariman Ten Zero. Yield about 1 per run.
- Best run: Halako Perimeter (Exterminate).
1. Run the Exterminate with a Loot Radar mod or Golden Instinct.
2. Scan any tone and finish.
- Items here: The Offering.

### Single-song planets: Mercury, Mars, Jupiter, Europa, Ceres, Eris, Sedna, Pluto, Neptune. Yield about 1 per run.
- Best run: an Exterminate on each planet, because it covers the most tileset. The planet picks the song, not the node.
1. Mercury (Vor's Prize): Pantheon or M Prime.
2. Mars (Consume Us): Hellas or Ultor.
3. Jupiter (The Profit): Carpo.
4. Europa (Origin System): Armaros or Baal.
5. Ceres (This Is What You Are): Pallas.
6. Eris (Our Disease): Saxis.
7. Sedna (Grineer Onslaught): Adaro.
8. Pluto (March Of The Moa): Narcissus or Acheron.
9. Neptune (Archwing): Neso.
- Items here: Vor's Prize, Consume Us, The Profit, Origin System, This Is What You Are, Our Disease, Grineer Onslaught, March Of The Moa, Archwing.

## Gotchas
- A scan only saves if you complete the mission. Landscapes save when you leave, and Duviri saves on a Decree or after the Orowyrm.
- Each tone scans once per mission. Once a song is done, extra tones stay in the tileset but show desaturated.
- A mission can hold a Somachord Tone alongside Cephalon and Frame Fighter Fragments. Somachord Tones do not count toward junction Cephalon Fragment totals.
- Vallis fixed tones count only toward a song already in progress, and once per visit. Scan a roaming tone first.
- Vallis roaming tones are invisible on the minimap while on a K-Drive, and the pink is hard to spot on snow.
- The Sacrifice and Call of the Tempestarii tones must be scanned in mission order. If you miss one, replay from that mission.
- Break Narmer Junk Run is only available one week in three.
- The Descendia tone has been reported invisible by at least one player.
- The Duviri Kullervo's Hold island and Amphitheater only exist in certain Spiral moods.
- Somachords not on this list (Varzia, Baro, Aoi, Teshin, Zorba, Koumei, Melica) are bought with currency, not scanned.

## Community sentiment
Orb Vallis is called the worst Somachord grind in the game and the 2019 rants about eleven-hour searches are why. Players now say the fixed Fortuna tone trick makes it about two to three hours with a Golden Instinct, and players hope the trick is never patched. Hidden-room tones like Kuva Fortress, Albrecht's Laboratories and the Phobos pair are described as spiteful. Quest tones are the other regret: players replay The Sacrifice and Call of the Tempestarii several times because the order requirement is easy to break.
