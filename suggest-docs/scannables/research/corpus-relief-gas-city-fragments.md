# Fragments: Corpus Relief and Gas City (Jupiter)

Two sets of Corpus lore fragments: the Tenets of Parvos Granum (eleven, paid for at a Temple Relief) and Alad V's Partnership fragments (eight scannable canisters in the Gas City). Completing them fills the codex lore entries and, for the Tenets, an Orbiter decoration per Tenet.

**Pain level: low for Gas City, moderate for Corpus Relief.** Corpus Relief is capped at one Tenet per run, because each run has one Treasurer, one Crown and at most one Relief room, so ten missing Tenets cost at least ten runs. Gas City has no cap, but fragment 1 sits in a rare tile that players report seeing a handful of times in dozens of runs.

## Gates
The Deadlock Protocol (Treasurers drop no Granum Crowns before it, so the Tenets are locked until it is done)

## Gear
- Helios with Investigator: scans canisters for you in the Gas City, and players report it picking up fragments they never saw. Common.
- Hildryn, Revenant and Vauban abilities (Pillage, Enthrall, Bastille, Minelayer coils) do not work on the Treasurer. Kill it with damage; it keeps running and drops tether grenades.
- Vacuum, Fetch and Pilfering Swarm do not pick up the Crown. Walk over it.

## Places
### Corpus Ship tileset (Temple Relief): Phobos, Venus, Mars, Neptune, Pluto, Jupiter Adrastea. Yield 1 per run.
- Best run: Phobos, Iliad (Assassination). Short, solo-friendly, and the Sergeant has a high chance of spawning in the Relief room, so the boss walks you to it. Phobos Roche (Exterminate) and Gulliver (Defense) are the fast fallbacks.
1. Load a Corpus Ship mission and play normally. Within 2 to 4 minutes (3 to 8 in endless missions) a Treasurer announces itself with a transmission and a red marker.
2. Kill the Treasurer and walk over the Crown it drops. Any tier works: the Tenets unlock in a fixed order whatever the Crown, so a basic Granum Crown from Venus, Mars or Phobos is enough.
3. Find the Relief room: a big open room with a Parvos statue on a pyramid and a fountain behind it. The console sits at the statue's base. Spend the Crown there.
4. Clear nearby enemies first. The console and the Crown spend refuse to work while enemies are close.
5. Finish the mission. Only one Relief room spawns per mission, and the Crown must be spent in the same mission it dropped.
- Items here: Giving Back, A New Beginning, Bury The Past, A New Empire, We Are Corpus, Crawling Back, The Gemstone, The Taking, To The City, What Right Do They Have?

### Corpus Gas City tileset (Partnership): Jupiter. Yield up to 8 per run, 1 to 3 in practice.
- Best run: Jupiter, Thebe (Sabotage). Short and solo-friendly. A secret lab tile spawns here and players report the second objective room, the one with only 2 objects to destroy, carries a fragment. Metis (Rescue) and Carpo (Exterminate) are the other nodes players report finding the rare fragment 1 on.
1. Load any Jupiter Corpus node: Metis, Callisto, Elara, Io, Carme, Ananke, Carpo, Amalthea, Thebe or Themisto. Sinai, Cameria and the other Infested Jupiter nodes are not Gas City.
2. Explore each tile and look for small violet Hexenon canisters. Scan each one once with the Codex Scanner.
3. The rare ones are in specific tiles: fragment 1 is in a tile with a big overhead gas tube; fragment 6 is in the open vista tile.
4. Do not leave a mission early if a tile with the overhead tube shows up. Players report that tile only every few dozen runs.
- Items here: The Partnership Fragment 1/8, The Partnership Fragment 2/8, The Partnership Fragment 3/8, The Partnership Fragment 4/8, The Partnership Fragment 5/8, The Partnership Fragment 6/8, The Partnership Fragment 7/8, The Partnership Fragment 8/8

## Gotchas
- One Crown, one Treasurer and one Relief room per mission. A solo run gives one Tenet.
- The Treasurer never spawns on Europa Valac (Crossfire), even though it uses the Corpus Ship tileset.
- No Treasurer spawns if a player is within 35 meters of extraction when it would appear, and a Crown dropped in the extraction zone is moved to a safe spot.
- Other squad members can spend their own Crown at the same console after the first plays and each get the Tenet, so a squad with Crowns multiplies the yield.
- Tenets always unlock in order, so the order of the table above is the order they arrive. Once all eleven are done, the Crown pays Credits instead.
- The Treasurer can also spawn on a Grineer Galleon node occupied by the Corpus.
- Fragments in the Gas City respect tile weights: some tiles are far more common on some mission types, and a lab or fragment tile may be absent from any given run.
- Fragment 1 is the one players get stuck on; a Helios will scan it before you see it, so check the codex after a run.

## Community sentiment
Tenets are seen as easy but slow: players run Phobos Iliad because the Sergeant lands in the Relief room. Gas City fragments are mostly fine, with fragment 1 as the one that takes dozens of runs.
