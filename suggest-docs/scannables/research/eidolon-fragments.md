# Eidolon Fragments (Thousand-Year Fish)

Twenty glass fish statues hidden across the Plains of Eidolon. Each scans once for a Codex entry with hidden Onkko narration, and scanning all twenty mails the Thousand-Year Fish Orbiter statuette.

**Pain level: moderate, one long walk.** Every fish sits at a fixed spot (nothing is random), so the only cost is finding and reaching them: roof-tops, caves, sentient bones, and one fish (Ostron Artisans) behind a mini-puzzle.

## Gates
- Vor's Prize (unlocks both Cetus nodes on Earth).

## Gear
- Helios with Investigator scans fish on its own when you pass near them (wiki; common among players). Best single help for the hidden and high-up ones.
- A frame that reaches rooftops without effort: Titania, Valkyr, Wukong, Zephyr (one Steam guide author, plus a recent r/Warframe commenter who used Titania and Helios on the hardest fish).
- The Synthesis Scanner's Cross-Matrix widget does not trigger on these fish, so it adds nothing here.

## Places
### Plains of Eidolon: Earth, via the Cetus node or the Cetus gate node. Yield 18 per run.
- Best run: Cetus, Earth, either node; both are the free-roam Plains with no timer and no objective. Run it in the day cycle so only Grineer are around. Leaving through the Cetus gates saves progress.
1. Enter the Plains and open the map. The fish are all fixed, so work the plains in a loop and use blinkpads to cut the walking.
2. Sentient bones and wreckage (fish sit on top, usually the tallest point): The Unum, The Tower's Flesh, The Ostrons, Remnants of Orokin, Plains of Eidolon, Tools of Harvest. Tools of Harvest is on a piece of debris out in the water.
3. Grineer structures (jump onto the roof or a support beam): The Quills of Cetus, Plains Animals, Grineer Excavations, Harvesting The Tower. Several of these moved a little when the New War remastered the Plains, so scan the whole roof or top of the structure rather than a remembered spot.
4. Caves (go to the back or the middle rock formation): Amps, Life In Cetus, Teralyst Eidolon. Life In Cetus is in a side crevice right behind the power core. The same caves count for the Tomb Looter achievement.
5. Rocks and edges: Ancient History is on a small rock island off the south-east coast. Cetus is on a rock in front of a shield pylon in the north-east. Merchants of Cetus is at the source of the river in the far north-west. The Grineer Tusks is under a fallen Orokin device.
6. Ostron Artisans (the step people get wrong): go to the orange Grineer water-treatment whirlpool south-west of Cetus. Shoot the windmill blades with the Imperial Rampart emplacement about 110 m north of it until they fold shut, then hack the console at the windmill base. The tank drains, and the fish is in one of the pipes inside.
- Items here: Amps, Ancient History, Cetus, Grineer Excavations, Harvesting The Tower, Life In Cetus, Merchants of Cetus, Ostron Artisans, Plains Animals, Plains of Eidolon, Remnants of Orokin, Teralyst Eidolon, The Grineer Tusks, The Ostrons, The Quills of Cetus, The Tower's Flesh, The Unum, Tools of Harvest.

## Gotchas
- Each fish scans once only and always sits in the same place, so a missed one costs only a return trip.
- The Ostron Artisans fish is awkward to scan even when drained; players report needing Helios, or Titania's pixy form, to land the scan.
- Old guide screenshots predate the New War remaster; several rooftop fish sit in slightly different places on the changed structures.
- Fish cannot be marked with a waypoint.

## Community sentiment
Players call the set tedious but not hard: no RNG, just climbing and looking. The most-quoted complaints are the rooftop and windmill fish, and older reports of fish that would not register a scan; a Helios on the run makes most of it a walk-by.
