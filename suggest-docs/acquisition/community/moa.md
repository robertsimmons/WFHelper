# MOA — Reddit farming summary

**Pain level: no RNG at all, but the single highest up-front cost of any companion in the game.** Nothing here is *hard* — it's expensive and slow, and you realistically need **four** of them.

## What you're actually signing up for
From the most detailed guide post, describing MOAs as "the most costly companions to get in the entire game, and it's not even close":
- **Fortuna rank 3 (Old Mate progression) minimum** — you need standing with Solaris United before Legs will sell you anything.
- **~4 separate builds**, each with a build timer, so **4+ days minimum, realistically about a week** accounting for resources.
- **Roughly 1.5 million credits** total, including refining minerals and assembly.
- Estimated **~30k Solaris standing** and **~40 Shelter Debt-Bonds**.

## Why four?
MOA **models (heads) determine precepts**, and each of the four models has at least one thing worth owning. Once built, precepts can be equipped on *any* MOA (max 2 at a time), so building all four is a one-time unlock exercise.

| Model | Precepts | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Nychus** | **Blast Shield**, Hard Engage | Blast Shield is *the* must-have — with Manifold Bond it lets the MOA abuse shield gating, making it arguably the tankiest companion in the game. Don't run a MOA without it. |
| **Para** | Whiplash Mine, Anti-Grav Grenade | Whiplash Mine is an excellent 20m grouping tool. Anti-Grav is niche/bad. |
| **Lambeo** | Stasis Field, Shockwave Actuators | Stasis Field = 60% DR vs enemy guns over a big area. Shockwave is a joke mod. |
| **Oloro** | **Security Override**, Tractor Beam | Auto-hacks consoles. The MOA's genuinely unique trick — great in Spy and Archon Spy missions. Tractor Beam is for laughs. |

**Hard Engage is widely panned** — one long comment details that it overrides the MOA's AI, has negative synergy with Manifold Bond by overwriting weapon damage types, and doesn't proc what the wiki says it does. Skip it.

## Part selection
- **Core and Gyro** affect base stats — the only thing that matters is hitting **343 base shields** for **Reinforced Bond**. (And if you run Blast Shield, even that stops mattering, since it pushes you over the threshold regardless — which frees up a lot of mod space.)
- **Bracket** = legs = polarity. **Tian gives D (Vazarin)**, **Gauth gives −**. Picking a useful one saves a Forma; otherwise it's fashion.
- **Model** is mostly fashion once you own all four.
- Weapons: any Sentinel weapon **except Deconstructor** — so Verglas and Helstrum are on the table.

## Fortuna standing (the real time cost)
Not deeply covered in the MOA threads, but the standing methods people cited:
- **Conservation** is the most-recommended — Loki for invisibility with **Rest & Rage subsumed** to sleep animals. **Rare Kubrodons give ~8k in about 5 minutes.**
- **Mining** works and some people prefer it, but it's slower.

## Known trade-offs
- **MOAs cannot use Resourceful Retriever** (the loot mods). For several people this is a hard dealbreaker and the reason they run beast companions exclusively.
- **They don't keep up with fast movement** — Titania pixie mode, Wukong cloud, etc. leave them behind, which undercuts the auto-hack advantage since you wait for them at the console anyway.
- **Personality modules (50p each) lower DPS** — the MOA plays emotes mid-combat and dies more. Universally acknowledged, and roughly half the thread said they don't care because it's charming. You can just keep a second loadout without the module.
- Counter-argument raised: **Nautilus groups better** and **Diriga primes better**, so the MOA's case is versatility (DR + grouping + auto-hack + spare mod slots), not being best-in-class at anything.

## Community sentiment
Overwhelmingly affectionate but self-aware — the big post is explicitly titled as an agenda and admits the acquisition cost up front "for the sake of transparency." Consensus: worth it, but do it when you have credits and standing to burn, not as a priority farm.
